How big are DToL plant genomes?

Each Darwin Tree of Life plant can be measured three ways: the length of its assembly, a k-mer estimate from GenomeScope, and flow cytometry. Where the three disagree, something interesting is usually going on, whether that is ploidy, repeats or a model that did not fit.

Worth a second look

Every species in the current view, most discordant first. Discordance is the largest fold difference between any two of the three estimates, so polyploids rank highly because GenomeScope reports a single set. Click a row to pick out that species in the plots, or a heading to sort.

Data come from the Darwin Tree of Life Plant Working Group's plant-sequencing-status pipeline and are read live each time this page opens (TSV). GenomeScope lengths are the upper bound of the model's haploid length, at k = 31.
