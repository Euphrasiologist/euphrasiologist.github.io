Collecting the British flora for the Darwin Tree of Life

The Darwin Tree of Life Plant Working Group is collecting every species on its target list of British vascular plants and bryophytes for genome sequencing. This dashboard reads the collectors' spreadsheets each time it opens, so it is always up to date.
